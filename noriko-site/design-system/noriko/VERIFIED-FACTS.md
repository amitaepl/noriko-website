**Two corrections against the launch deck.** The deck labelled the 102 g pack
"Big Latiao"; the final label calls it **Mini Latiao**. The deck called the second
konjac "Sour & Hot"; the label says **Sour & Spicy**. The labels win.

**Imagery note.** Neither `FINAL LABEL FILES WEILONG` nor `REVISED LABELS 21MAY`
contains front-of-pack artwork — both hold only the regulatory back panel. The
packshots on the site are cropped from the launch deck's product photography,
using per-product boxes read off the pages. Automated detection was abandoned:
the saturated-colour mask misses the white pouch bodies, and the pages carry
bamboo-leaf decoration in the same hue as the Kiss Burn and Konjac Sour packs,
so blobs merged. Hand-read boxes are in the build notes and are deterministic.
Three of the five are white pouches shot on white with a soft shadow: they cannot
be cut out to transparency without eating into the pack, so they are kept on a
white ground, which is invisible on the white snack cards. **Front-of-pack
packshots on transparent or plain ground would be worth requesting.**

**Prices not shown on the site.** These five carry verified MRPs, but the other
eight snacks do not, so showing price on some cards and not others would read as
an omission. Weight and heat rating carry every card.

**Pricing note.** MRPs in the verified range run ₹150–₹500. "₹30" is therefore
**not** a range-wide fact and must never be used as Snack's brand identity. The
homepage shows no snack prices at all; weights and heat ratings carry the cards.

**⚠ Conflict to resolve — the nutrition panel is identical on all five labels.**

**⚠ Two further artwork defects, not reproduced on the site.**
1. **"Manufactured By: xxxyyyzzz"** — an unfilled placeholder on all five final

2. The MSG warning reads "NOT RECOMMENDED FOR INFANTS BELOW **-12** MONTHS" — a
   stray hyphen. The site renders "below 12 months"; the artwork should be fixed.

Also present on the labels and deliberately **not** used on the retail product
pages: HSN 19059030 and GST 5%. These are trade data and belong to HoReCa, not
to a consumer page. The FSSAI licence (10021011000329) *is* shown, as it is a
consumer-facing statutory detail.

**No back-of-pack artwork exists for the other 25 retail SKUs** — the eight
NORIKO fish/squid/tofu/lotus snacks and all seventeen Cook products. Their
product pages show a labelled gap in place of ingredients and nutrition rather
than any substitute text. Obtaining these labels is now a launch requirement.

## Dish imagery — provenance, 28 Aug 2026

**28 pack-and-dish images**, supplied by the brand in two batches. Each shows the
pack beside a plated idea.

**These are AI-generated brand images, not photography of the product.** The
supplied filenames are ChatGPT exports, all 1536×1024. They are therefore:

- captioned **"Serving suggestion"** on product pages, never "what it makes";
- recorded per product in `products.json` as `dish_source`;
- used for the card pack→dish hover crossfade, with `dish_focus` panning the
  4:5 card crop hard right so the food fills the card and the pack does not.

Converted to WebP at 1800px-wide max, 3:2, via `tools/import-dish-images.py` —
2.1–3 MB PNGs down to roughly 150–330 KB each. Originals kept outside the repo
in `NORIKO WEBSITE/food images/`.

**Coverage: all 32 Cook SKUs.** **None of the 13 Snack products has one.**

**Nori Sheet 10 and 50 share one image**, supplied that way by the brand owner.
The pack in frame states "Net Contents 50 sheets", so on the 10-sheet product
page the hover shows a 50-sheet pack. The alt text there names what is actually
shown rather than implying a 10-sheet pack, and `dish_note` records it. A
10-sheet frame would remove the discrepancy.
