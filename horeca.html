    Before you enquire

    Common questions from professional buyers.

    Do you publish prices online?

    No. Prices are not published online. Add the products you need to the enquiry list and send it, and our team replies with availability and pricing.

    What are the minimum order, delivery to my city and samples?

    These depend on the product and your location, so our team confirms them with your quote. Mention your city and what you need in the enquiry and we will come back with the details.

    What pack sizes and case configurations do you have?

    The product list on this page shows each professional pack size and its case configuration, for example 12 bags per case. Filter by category and add items to your enquiry.

    Can I get the HoReCa catalogue?

    Yes. Enter your business email in the catalogue form on this page and we will email you the catalogue link.

    How else can I reach the team?

    Message the helpline on WhatsApp at +91 89282 27122, or email info@aepl.biz.

    Who is behind NORIKO?

    NORIKO is a brand of Ashokasha Exim Pvt. Ltd., a specialist importer and distributor of Asian food products.
