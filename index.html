    About NORIKO

    One brand, across the Asian table.

    Founded in 2022, NORIKO brings together Asian cooking ingredients, ready-to-eat snacks and JUZO popping boba drinks for India.

    Owned by Ashokasha Exim Pvt. Ltd., NORIKO serves both professional kitchens and modern retail, with one clear idea: make Asian food easier to cook, serve, discover and enjoy.
