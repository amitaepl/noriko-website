Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information NORIKO collects through noriko.in, why we collect it, and your choices. NORIKO is a brand of Ashokasha Exim Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"), based in Thane West 400604, Maharashtra, India.

What we collect

  • Enquiry and contact forms. Name, company or firm name, email address, phone number, the products or ranges you are interested in, and your message.
  • Catalogue request. Your business email address, so we can send you the HoReCa catalogue link.
  • WhatsApp. If you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a message already written. We only receive what you choose to send.
  • Website usage. With Google Analytics 4 we see anonymous visit data such as pages viewed, device type, approximate location and which buttons are tapped. It does not tell us who you are.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, send pricing and availability, and send the catalogue you asked for.
  • To understand which pages and products are useful, and to improve the website.

We do not sell your information. We do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who handles it for us

  • Formspree receives the enquiry and contact forms and passes them to us by email and WhatsApp alert.
  • Resend delivers the catalogue email.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Google provides Analytics and Search Console.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the messages you send us on WhatsApp.

These providers may process information outside India. We only share with them what is needed to run the service.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies to count visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out add-on. The site works without them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your request and for our ordinary business records, and then delete them. Ask us if you want yours removed sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw consent to us contacting you. Write to info@aepl.biz or +91 89282 27122. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is meant for adults and for business buyers. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it last changed.

Grievance officer

If you have a concern about how your information is handled, write to our Grievance Officer, Ms. Asawari Gujar, Ashokasha Exim Pvt. Ltd., Thane West 400604, Maharashtra, India, at compliance@aepl.biz or +91 89282 27122. We will acknowledge your complaint and respond within a reasonable time.
