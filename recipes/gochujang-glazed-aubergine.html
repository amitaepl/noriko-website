Gochujang Glazed Aubergine

Soft roasted aubergine in a sticky, sweet-spicy gochujang glaze, best over rice.

Prep 10 min Cook 25 min Serves 3 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 2 large aubergines, cut into thick wedges
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 tbsp NORIKO Gochujang
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp jaggery or brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp NORIKO Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • Toasted sesame seeds and spring onion, to finish
  • Cooked rice, to serve

Method

  1. Roast. Toss the aubergine in oil and roast at 220°C for 15 to 18 minutes until soft and golden.
  2. Mix the glaze. Stir gochujang, soy, sugar, vinegar, sesame oil, garlic and 2 tbsp water.
  3. Glaze. Brush the glaze over the aubergine and roast 5 to 7 minutes more until sticky.
  4. Finish. Scatter sesame and spring onion.
  5. Serve. Spoon over hot rice with any glaze left in the tray.
Chef's note: Watch the last few minutes, since the sugar in the glaze browns quickly.
