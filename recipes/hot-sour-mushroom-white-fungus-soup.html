Hot & Sour Mushroom and White Fungus Soup

A warming, tangy egg-drop soup with soft mushrooms and delicate white fungus.

Prep 15 min Cook 20 min Serves 4 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 10 g NORIKO Dried Shiitake Mushroom, soaked in warm water for 30 minutes
  • 15 g NORIKO White Fungus, soaked for 20 minutes and torn
  • 150 g fresh mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 carrot, cut into thin sticks
  • 1 litre vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp NORIKO Chinkiang Vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 3 tbsp water
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 tsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced

Method

  1. Soak. Soak the shiitake and white fungus. Slice the shiitake and keep the soaking water.
  2. Simmer. Bring the stock and strained soaking water to a simmer with both mushrooms and the carrot for 10 minutes.
  3. Season. Add soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and white pepper.
  4. Thicken. Stir in the cornflour mixture and simmer 2 minutes until lightly thick.
  5. Add the egg. Slowly pour in the beaten eggs in a thin stream while stirring gently, so they set into soft ribbons.
  6. Finish. Add the sesame oil and spring onion and serve hot.
Chef's note: Add the vinegar and pepper to taste at the end for your preferred balance of hot and sour.
