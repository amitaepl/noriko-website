Kimchi Fried Rice

A fast, punchy rice bowl with vegetables, sesame and a crisp-edged fried egg.

Prep 10 min Cook 15 min Serves 2 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked, chilled rice
  • 3 tbsp NORIKO Kimchi Sauce
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 small carrot, diced
  • ½ cup shredded cabbage
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 eggs
  • Sesame seeds and NORIKO Nori, to finish

Method

  1. Mix the sauce. Stir together kimchi sauce, soy and sesame oil.
  2. Fry the vegetables. Heat neutral oil in a wok. Cook carrot and cabbage over high heat for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the rice. Break in the cold rice and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until hot and separate.
  4. Season. Add the sauce and most of the spring onion. Toss until evenly red and glossy.
  5. Finish. Fry the eggs separately. Divide the rice between bowls and top with egg, sesame, nori and remaining spring onion.
Chef's note: Cold, day-old rice fries cleanly and stays pleasantly separate.
