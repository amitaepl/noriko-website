Sesame Soba Salad

Cool buckwheat noodles, crisp vegetables and a bright, nutty dressing.

Prep 15 min Cook 10 min Serves 3 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 250 g NORIKO Soba Noodles
  • 1 cucumber, ribboned
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup cooked edamame
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp NORIKO Rice Vinegar
  • 1½ tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • Sesame seeds and chilli, to finish

Method

  1. Make the dressing. Whisk vinegar, soy, sesame oil and maple syrup.
  2. Cook the soba. Boil until just tender, then rinse thoroughly under cold water.
  3. Dry well. Shake off excess water so the dressing is not diluted.
  4. Assemble. Toss noodles with cucumber, carrot, edamame, spring onion and dressing.
  5. Serve. Finish with sesame and chilli; chill briefly or serve at once.
Chef's note: Rinsing removes excess starch and keeps soba light and springy.
