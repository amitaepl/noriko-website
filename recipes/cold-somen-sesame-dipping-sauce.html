Cold Somen with Sesame Dipping Sauce

Thin chilled noodles with a nutty, tangy dipping sauce. Ready in under 15 minutes.

Prep 10 min Cook 4 min Serves 2 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 200 g NORIKO Somen Noodles
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp NORIKO Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tbsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 small cucumber, cut into thin sticks
  • 1 sheet NORIKO Nori, cut into strips
  • A little NORIKO Prepared Wasabi, to serve

Method

  1. Make the sauce. Stir soy, vinegar, sesame oil, sugar and 3 tbsp water until the sugar dissolves. Add the sesame seeds.
  2. Boil the noodles. Cook the somen in plenty of boiling water for 2 to 3 minutes, or as the pack says.
  3. Chill. Drain, rinse under cold running water and drain again so the noodles are cool and not sticky.
  4. Plate. Pile the noodles on plates with cucumber, spring onion and nori.
  5. Serve. Dip each forkful in the sauce, adding wasabi to taste.
Chef's note: Rinsing well is what keeps somen light and separate.
