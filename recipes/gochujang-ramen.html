Gochujang Ramen

A deeply savoury, gently spicy broth with springy noodles, greens and jammy eggs.

Prep 10 min Cook 20 min Serves 2 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 2 portions NORIKO Ramen Noodles
  • 2 tbsp NORIKO Gochujang
  • 750 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 pak choi
  • NORIKO Nori, mushrooms and spring onion

Method

  1. Cook the eggs. Boil for 7 minutes, cool in iced water, peel and halve.
  2. Build the broth. Simmer stock with gochujang, soy, sesame oil, garlic and ginger for 8 minutes.
  3. Add vegetables. Simmer mushrooms and pak choi until just tender.
  4. Cook noodles. Add ramen and cook according to the packet directions.
  5. Serve. Divide between bowls and finish with egg, nori and spring onion.
Chef's note: Taste the broth before adding extra salt—gochujang and soy already provide seasoning.
