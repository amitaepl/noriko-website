Bamboo & Water Chestnut Stir-Fry

Crisp vegetables in a light ginger, soy and sesame glaze.

Prep 15 min Cook 10 min Serves 3 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 200 g NORIKO Bamboo Shoots, drained
  • 200 g NORIKO Water Chestnuts, drained
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 150 g snow peas
  • 150 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp NORIKO Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Method

  1. Mix the glaze. Combine soy, vinegar and sesame oil.
  2. Heat the wok. Add neutral oil over high heat until shimmering.
  3. Start firm vegetables. Fry mushrooms, bamboo shoots and water chestnuts for 3 minutes.
  4. Add colour. Add pepper, snow peas, garlic and ginger; toss for 2 minutes.
  5. Glaze. Pour in the sauce and toss for one minute. Serve immediately.
Chef's note: Keep the wok hot and cooking brief so every vegetable retains its crunch.
