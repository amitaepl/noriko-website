Tempura Vegetables with Wasabi Mayo

Light, crisp tempura vegetables with a creamy wasabi dip.

Prep 15 min Cook 15 min Serves 4 Medium
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 150 g tempura batter mix
  • 220 ml ice-cold water
  • 1 sweet potato, sliced into thin rounds
  • 1 red pepper, cut into strips
  • 100 g green beans, trimmed
  • 1 onion, cut into rings
  • Oil, for deep frying
  • 4 tbsp NORIKO Mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp NORIKO Prepared Wasabi
  • 1 tsp NORIKO Rice Vinegar

Method

  1. Make the dip. Mix mayonnaise, wasabi and vinegar and chill.
  2. Heat the oil. Bring about 8 cm of oil to 180°C.
  3. Mix the batter. Stir the batter mix with ice-cold water until just combined. Lumps are fine.
  4. Fry in batches. Dip the vegetables, fry 2 to 4 minutes until pale golden, and drain on paper.
  5. Serve at once. Eat while crisp with the wasabi mayo.
Chef's note: Cold batter and a light hand give the crispest coating.
