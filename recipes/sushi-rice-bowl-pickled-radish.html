Sushi Rice Bowl with Pickled Radish & Ginger

Seasoned sushi rice topped with crisp vegetables, tangy pickled radish and sushi ginger.

Prep 15 min Cook 20 min Serves 3 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 300 g NORIKO Sushi Rice
  • 3 tbsp NORIKO Sushi Vinegar
  • 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, cut into thin sticks
  • 100 g NORIKO Pickled Radish (Sushi Takuan), sliced
  • 2 tbsp NORIKO Sushi Ginger
  • 1 sheet NORIKO Nori, shredded
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Light soy sauce and NORIKO Prepared Wasabi, to serve

Method

  1. Cook the rice. Rinse until the water runs nearly clear, then cook with 360 ml water and rest covered for 10 minutes.
  2. Season. Fold in the sushi vinegar with a cutting motion and let the rice cool to warm.
  3. Prepare toppings. Slice the cucumber, carrot and radish.
  4. Build the bowls. Spoon rice into bowls and arrange the toppings in sections.
  5. Finish. Scatter nori and sesame, add ginger, and serve with soy and wasabi.
Chef's note: Fold gently so the grains stay whole and do not turn mushy.
