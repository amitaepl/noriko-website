Sesame Shiitake Udon

Chewy noodles and savoury mushrooms coated in a glossy garlic-sesame sauce.

Prep 15 min Cook 15 min Serves 2 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 2 packs NORIKO Udon Noodles
  • 20 g NORIKO Dried Shiitake
  • 2 small pak choi
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp NORIKO Rice Vinegar
  • 2 tsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • Spring onion and sesame, to finish

Method

  1. Soak. Cover shiitake with hot water for 20 minutes, then slice. Reserve 60 ml soaking liquid.
  2. Mix. Combine soy, vinegar, sesame oil, sugar and reserved liquid.
  3. Prepare noodles. Loosen udon according to the packet directions and drain.
  4. Stir-fry. Heat neutral oil. Fry mushrooms for 3 minutes, then add pak choi and garlic.
  5. Toss. Add udon and sauce; toss over high heat for 2 minutes. Finish with spring onion and sesame.
Chef's note: The strained mushroom soaking liquid gives the sauce extra depth.
