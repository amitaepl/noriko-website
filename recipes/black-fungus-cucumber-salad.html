Black Fungus Cucumber Salad

Springy black fungus and cool cucumber in a tangy chilli-sesame dressing.

Prep 25 min Cook 10 min Serves 4 Easy
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • 30 g NORIKO Dried Black Fungus
  • 1 large cucumber
  • 2 tbsp NORIKO Rice Vinegar
  • 1½ tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp NORIKO Sesame Oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 red chilli, sliced
  • ½ cup coriander leaves
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Rehydrate. Soak fungus in warm water for 20 minutes until fully expanded; trim any firm bases.
  2. Blanch. Boil for 3 minutes, drain and cool completely.
  3. Prepare cucumber. Lightly smash, then cut into bite-sized pieces.
  4. Dress. Mix vinegar, soy, sesame oil, sugar, garlic and chilli.
  5. Toss. Combine everything with coriander and sesame. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.
Chef's note: Drain the fungus thoroughly so the dressing clings instead of becoming watery.
