# COOK — page overrides

Inherits `../MASTER.md`. Only the deviations are listed here.

**Reference lineage:** Numata Nori (spacing, heading/body ratio, plain provenance),
Clearspring (ingredient-word tiles, light large headings), Sempio (purpose line per line).

## Surface
- Ground alternates `--paper` and `--cook-deep` (`#16362C`) for editorial bands.
- On the deep green, body text uses `rgba(255,255,255,.82)`; headings pure white.
- Accent stays `--noriko-red`. No secondary accent — restraint is the point.

## Type
- Push the Numata ratio: headings one step larger than master scale, body unchanged.
  Section heads at `clamp(2.25rem, 4.5vw, 3.75rem)`.
- Ingredient names and pull quotes in `--font-serif` italic.

## Density
- Section padding `--space-10` on desktop (one step more than master). This world
  is allowed to breathe more than any other.

## Components
- Product card: one line saying **what the ingredient is and what it is used for**.
  Describe use, not invented flavour. "A mild cooking vinegar, used to season rice"
  is verifiable; "clean, mild and slightly sweet" is a tasting note nobody supplied.
  Real flavour copy can replace these once the brand team writes and approves it.
- Every category page opens with a one-line purpose statement (Sempio pattern).
- Ingredient guide blocks: what it is → what it does → what to make with it.
- The dish layer must show **genuine usage** of that specific product. Until the
  photography exists it stays a labelled `.needs-photo` placeholder.

## Avoid
- Snack flavour-band colours and JUZO fruit colours never appear in Cook.
- Any claim about a product beyond what the pack or catalogue states.
- Substituting an unrelated packshot as the "dish" image — it must show real usage.
- Exclamation marks, urgency, discount framing.
